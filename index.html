02 NeuroSuite · Why it was created

NeuroSuite brings order in the available signals.

In healthcare contexts there are many events. An orderly reading is needed, continuous and shareable of what happens over time.

NeuroSuite connects environmental signals, habits, operational demands and context variations into a simple, verifiable and supervised readout.

NeuroSuite operates as an observational support system: it collects and organizes useful information, highlights potentially relevant changes and makes them available to the people involved. Clinical assessment remains exclusively the responsibility of authorized healthcare professionals, while decisions remain in the hands of those responsible for care and assistance.

The objective is to support operators and caregivers in their daily work, making signals, changes and priorities that deserve attention more visible.

06 Scientific foundation

Digital and neurofunctional signals. An already active line of research.

NeuroSuite is part of a scientific line that studies neurophysiological, linguistic, motor and digital signals. The system uses them as observational and contextual elements within a supervised path.

WHAT NEUROSUITE DOES

Operational portable EEG connector

NeuroSuite instantly acquires and stores a trace through non-invasive dry-electrode portable electroencephalography, ready in about three seconds. The nine-minute measurement supports analysis, individual baseline creation and longitudinal monitoring, without diagnostic purposes.

PORTABLE EEG · METHOD

Krigolson et al. (2017)

The study validated a specific low-cost portable system for ERP research. It supports methodological feasibility; it does not validate NeuroSuite or clinical diagnosis.

RESTING-STATE EEG · REVIEW

Cassani et al. (2018)

The systematic review examines resting-state EEG in Alzheimer's disease diagnosis and progression. Heterogeneous methods prevent transferring its results directly to NeuroSuite.

PARKINSON'S · QEEG

Caviness et al. (2016)

The study reports quantitative EEG differences across a defined Parkinsonian cognitive spectrum. It supports research relevance, not individual prediction by NeuroSuite.

NARRATIVE LANGUAGE

Fraser, Meltzer & Rudzicz (2016)

Computational features distinguished research speech samples in a specific dataset. This is scientific context, not evidence that NeuroSuite currently diagnoses cognitive disease.

GAIT · LONGITUDINAL RISK

Verghese et al. (2002)

In a prospective cohort of older adults, neurological gait abnormalities were associated with later non-Alzheimer's dementia. Association does not establish diagnosis or causality.

08 Potential verticals

Prolonged operational stress

Contextual and supervised observation of fatigue, overload and changes in operational continuity.
