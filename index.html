01 Idea

Beyond a single signal.
The relationships between things.

A person. A place. A process. Every context has its own patterns of change.

NeuroSuite is a multimodal, multi-domain ecosystem. It connects authorised information over time, develops context-appropriate references and brings meaningful changes to the right person’s attention.

Nina and other interfaces make this understanding accessible. Behind them is a broader engine: a shared approach, adapted to distinct contexts with independent boundaries and responsibilities.

04 Progress

Built, being tested.
A clear direction.

Working prototypes

Personal applications and engine components have been developed and undergo technical checks. AgainHere is in private testing.

Context-specific development

Each application needs its own connectors, rules, permissions and evidence. A shared architecture does not make every domain operational.

Research & validation

Research hypotheses remain distinct from demonstrated functions. No claims of diagnosis or clinical prediction.

05 Privacy

Connected in approach.
Separate in data.

My Lifeline and AgainHere personal data remain separate from other domains. They do not feed care institutions, employers, retail or other verticals. Each context maintains its own purposes, permissions and access.

The value is not knowing everything.
It is making what matters visible.
