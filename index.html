01 Idea

Beyond a single signal.
The relationships between things.

A person. A place. A process. Every context has its own patterns of change.

NeuroSuite is a modular, multimodal ecosystem. It organises authorised signals over time, establishes individual or context-specific baselines when data quality is sufficient, and highlights persistent or converging change that deserves review.

Nina and domain interfaces make results accessible. Shared analysis and correlation capabilities support distinct modules, while data, purpose, permissions and evidence remain separated for every context.

02 Ecosystem

One shared core.
Different horizons.

The project combines implemented components, controlled prototypes and documented development lines. Their maturity is stated separately: this is not a catalogue of commercially validated products.

04 Progress

Built, being tested.
A clear direction.

Verified software foundation

Longitudinal components, provenance controls, baseline handling, review controls, mobile and sensor ingestion paths, and segregated data boundaries have source-level and controlled-test evidence.

Controlled development & pilots

Android variants are tested in QA or prepared for supervised technical pilots. The iOS secure client, NeuroMotor and NeuroGym, and further vertical integrations remain in development and require device, field, regulatory and production gates.

Qualified industrial dialogue

The modular approach has received positive assessment and qualified professional and industrial interest. These dialogues support commercial exploration; they are not announced partnerships, certifications or market validation.

05 Privacy

Connected in approach.
Separate in data.

Personal, care, workplace, retail and research domains remain separated by purpose, environment, permissions and access. NeuroSuite applies data minimisation, provenance, revocation, auditability and security by design. These public pages collect no health, wearable or sensor data; controlled applications require their own notices, consents and governance.

The value is not knowing everything.
